Ask us directly →
What products and services does Rainham Windows offer?

Rainham Windows offers made-to-measure double glazing, replacement uPVC windows, doors, conservatories, porches and bi-fold doors for London homes. Contact the team to discuss the options for your property.

Where is Rainham Windows based, and which areas does it cover?

Rainham Windows lists its address as 105 Silver Street, London, N18 1RG and serves customers across London. If you are searching for double glazing near you, share your postcode and project details to check arrangements for your area.

Does Rainham Windows offer replacement uPVC windows?

Rainham Windows offers replacement uPVC windows. The original project portfolio includes anthracite-grey and rosewood uPVC window installations. Ask the team which styles and finishes are available for your home.

How much does double glazing in London cost?

A double-glazing quotation depends on the number and size of windows or doors, the chosen materials and the installation requirements. Rainham Windows offers free quotations: share your project details to get a price for your home.

How can I request a quotation?

Call 07460 713813 or email gagzz.sanghu@hotmail.co.uk to talk about what you need and request a free quotation.

Can I ask about replacing just one window or door?

Yes. Contact Rainham Windows with details of the work you have in mind, whether it is one item or a larger home improvement project.

What should I include when I get in touch?

Your London postcode, the type of product you are interested in and a brief description of your home are useful things to share in your first conversation.
